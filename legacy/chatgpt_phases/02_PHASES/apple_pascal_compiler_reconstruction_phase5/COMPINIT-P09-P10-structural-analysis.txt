APPLE PASCAL 1.1 — COMPINIT PROCEDURES 9 AND 10
Structural P-code analysis

IMPORTANT CORRECTION
--------------------
COMPINIT does NOT contain only ten procedures. The segment contains 29
procedures. Earlier reports truncated the procedure map at procedure 10.
The JTAB parser now finds all 29. This report analyzes procedures 9 and 10.

P9
--
Entry $0830
Exit  $0864
Data  2 bytes
Params 6 bytes
Lexical level 1

Raw:
0830 DA CC 04 DB F9 01 C3 A1 0A
0839 D9 DB FC 9A B9 21
0840 D9 DB FB 9F CB A1 08
0847 D8 DA FB A3 09 9A B9 03
084E D8 00 9A AD

Using TommyGoog's notation, DA/DB/D9/D8 are short SLDL forms for
local/parameter words. F9/FB are indirect-byte/field accesses and STO
performs an indirect assignment.

The first basic block is structurally:

    MP04 := MP03;
    IF <byte/field derived from MP04> = 1 THEN
    BEGIN
        <indirect assignment involving MP02 and MP04>;
        GOTO common_exit;
    END;

The second block compares an indirect value with a local/address-related
quantity and conditionally performs a byte assignment. The exact source
types are intentionally unresolved because the distinction between pointer,
VAR parameter, and packed/record field access cannot safely be determined
from P9 alone.

P10
---
Entry $0870
Exit  $0881
Data  0 bytes
Params 6 bytes
Lexical level 1

Raw:
0870 DA F8 D9 C9 A1 08
0876 DA DA F8 01 82 9A B9 03
087F D8 00 CF 02 AD

The first sequence has the recognizable form:

    IF MP02 < MP03^ THEN
        MP03^ := MP03^ + 1
    ELSE
        <alternate action>;

The exact meaning of the alternate action is not yet promoted to Pascal
source because CF 02 is a global-procedure call and the actual declaration
has not yet been recovered.

This is a strong clue that P10 is a small comparison/update helper rather
than generic initialization.

CALL-GRAPH NEXT STEP
--------------------
We should find every CLP 9 and CLP 10 in COMPINIT and inspect the stack
immediately before each call. That will reveal the actual parameter types.

The source-level reconstruction should not be finalized until those call
sites agree.

VALIDATION RULE
---------------
TommyGoog's work establishes that matching the visible semantics is not
enough: the reconstructed Pascal must eventually generate matching P-code.
His DeCompiler walks the JTAB-selected procedure and decodes the P-code,
and his re-engineering workflow uses Apple Pascal 1.1 to compile the
reconstructed source and compare generated P-code.
